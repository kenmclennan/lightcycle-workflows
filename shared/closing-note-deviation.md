**Required closing note.** The closing `--note` is never skipped: it names, in one sentence, any deviation from the spec (from BRIEF in `scope-and-code`, from SPEC in `build-workflow`), saying what was asked and what was built instead, or states "no deviation from the spec" ("no deviation from BRIEF" in `scope-and-code`, "no deviation from SPEC" in `build-workflow`) when there is none.
