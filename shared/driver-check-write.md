A bullet only the driver can measure - a live `lc` command a worker may not run, output only CI produces, a deployed system's behaviour - is written as `(driver, at review): <what the driver runs or observes, and the pass condition>`, and never leaves who measures it unsaid, since a coder otherwise either claims a result nobody measured or leaves the bullet silently unmet. The spec tells the coder to write `pending driver measurement` for each such bullet in the PR body.
